Hell is a contemporary opera with libretto by Eileen Myles, inspired by Dante's Inferno. A soldier named Lewis has been dying in battle for two thousand years. Guided through the underworld by a character named Brine, the opera employs frank, lyrical language and an exalted baroque style to tear away the veils obscuring corporate silence and global disaster. The music takes the rhythms of speech as its point of departure, changing speed and meter every second or two, building an almost constant stream of melody out of fragments of vernacular music. A chamber ensemble of strings, harpsichord, and vibraphone accompanies the singers.
